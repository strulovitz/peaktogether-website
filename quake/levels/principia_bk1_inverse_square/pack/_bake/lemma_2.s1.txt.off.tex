\large\bfseries
Newton considers a plane figure $AacE$ bounded by the straight side Aa,
the baseline AE, and the curve acE.
The baseline is divided into the equal bases $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, so that the base
is partitioned into segments of one common breadth.
The whole question concerns the area enclosed beneath the curve —
for it is this curve that is the heart of the figure and of the Lemma.
\end{varwidth}

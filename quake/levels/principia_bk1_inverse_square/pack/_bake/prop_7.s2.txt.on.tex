At the body's place we draw the \textcolor{tanteal}{tangent $PRZ$}, the forceless straight path the body would keep      without any centripetal pull — this is the heart of the deviation argument.      Through the force centre we draw the \textcolor{diampurple}{chord $PV$}, and through $V$ the \textcolor{diampurple}{diameter $VA$}, joining $AP$.      Because $VA$ is a diameter, the angle $VPA$ inscribed in the semicircle is a right angle,      and this single fact seeds the chain of similar triangles that will follow.      The \textcolor{diampurple}{chord $PV$ through $S$} is the intrinsic length that will ultimately govern the whole force law.

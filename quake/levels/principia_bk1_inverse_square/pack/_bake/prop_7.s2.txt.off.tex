At the body's place we draw the tangent $PRZ$, the forceless straight path the body would keep      without any centripetal pull — this is the heart of the deviation argument.      Through the force centre we draw the chord $PV$, and through $V$ the diameter $VA$, joining $AP$.      Because $VA$ is a diameter, the angle $VPA$ inscribed in the semicircle is a right angle,      and this single fact seeds the chain of similar triangles that will follow.      The chord $PV$ through $S$ is the intrinsic length that will ultimately govern the whole force law.

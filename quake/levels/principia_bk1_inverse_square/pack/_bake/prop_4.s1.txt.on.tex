\large\bfseries
The \textcolor{force}{centripetal force} — Newton's \textcolor{force}{pull toward the centre} — scales as the \textcolor{speed}{square of the speed} divided by the \textcolor{radius}{radius}. Double the \textcolor{speed}{speed} and the \textcolor{force}{pull} quadruples; double the \textcolor{radius}{radius} and the \textcolor{force}{pull} halves. Here the \textcolor{speed}{$v^2$} is the heart: it is the \textcolor{speed}{square}, not the speed itself, that governs how hard the body is drawn inward. Thus \textcolor{force}{$F$} answers to \textcolor{speed}{$v^2$} directly and to \textcolor{radius}{$r$} inversely.

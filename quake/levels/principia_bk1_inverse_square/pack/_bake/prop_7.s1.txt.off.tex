Proposition VII is the first concrete application of the force-measure proved in Proposition VI,      now applied to the simplest curvilinear orbit: the circle $VQPA$.      A body $P$ revolves in the circumference and $Q$ is the next place into which it moves.      The remarkable freedom here is that the force centre $S$ need not sit at the centre $O$ —      it may lie anywhere inside the circle, and we still seek the law along the radius $SP$,      which is the distance whose powers Prop. VI taught us to track.

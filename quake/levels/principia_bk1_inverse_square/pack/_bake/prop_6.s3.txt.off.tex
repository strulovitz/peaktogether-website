Here is Newton's decisive stroke: draw $QR$ PARALLEL to the radius $SP$ rather than straight to      the tangent, and this parallel is one heart of the construction. Then drop $QT$ PERPENDICULAR to      $SP$, the second heart, giving a right angle at $T$. Because $QR\parallel SP$, the versed sine now speaks      directly in the language of the force's own direction. And because $QT\perp SP$, the triangle $SQP$ has      area $\tfrac{1}{2}\,SP\cdot QT$ — which, by Prop. I, is proportional to the time in which the arc is swept.

\large\bfseries
Newton has proven the inverse-square law for the ellipse. Now he treats      the parabola — the limiting orbit that opens to infinity. A body at $P$      feels a force drawn to the focus $S$, and $SP$ is the radius      joining them. The question: does this open orbit still yield $F \propto 1/SP^2$?      Newton answers yes, by the same Prop. VI machinery.

\large\bfseries
The Law sets the alteration of motion in strict proportion to the motive force impressed. As Newton explains, if any force generates a motion, then a double force will generate double the motion, a triple force triple the motion — whether that force be impressed altogether and at once, or gradually and successively. The change $\Delta(\text{motion})$ scales exactly with the impressed $\mathbf{F}$.

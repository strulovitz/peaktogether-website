\large\bfseries
The alteration of motion is ever proportional to the motive force impress'd: "if any force generates a motion, a double force will generate double the motion," and a triple force triple the motion. This holds whether the force be impress'd altogether and at once, or gradually and successively. The exact scaling is the heart of the law — $\Delta(\text{motion})\ \propto F\$ — the seed from which $F = ma$ grows.

Repeat the same argument at $C$, $D$, $E$, and on: each centripetal impulse $Cc$, always parallel      to the radius $SB$, preserves the swept area, so $SCD = SBC$, $SDE = SCD$, and every triangle equals every      other. By composition the whole swept region $SADFS$ grows exactly as the time elapses. Now let the      number of triangles increase without bound and their breadth shrink to nothing: by \textbf{Lemma V, Cor.~4} the      ultimate perimeter $ADF$ becomes a smooth curve, the pulsing thumps merge into a force acting      \emph{continually}, and the equal-area rule passes unbroken to the limit — $\dfrac{dA}{dt} = \text{const}$.      This holds for \emph{any} central force, however it varies with distance: the force sculpts the \emph{shape} of      the orbit, but never touches the sacred equal-area law. \textit{Q.E.D.}

Now join $A$ to $B$ by the straight chord $AB$, a shortcut that cuts beneath the arc. Imagine the point $B$ now sliding down the curve toward $A$. As $B$ draws nearer, the chord $AB$ grows shorter and shorter, and its direction settles into the direction of the curve at $A$. It is this swivelling of the chord that the next step compares against the tangent.

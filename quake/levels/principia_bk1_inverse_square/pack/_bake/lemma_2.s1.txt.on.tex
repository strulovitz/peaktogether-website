\large\bfseries
Newton considers a plane figure $AacE$ bounded by the straight side \textcolor{base}{Aa},
the baseline \textcolor{base}{AE}, and the curve \textcolor{curve}{acE}.
The baseline is divided into the equal bases $AB$, $BC$, $CD$, so that \textcolor{base}{the base}
is partitioned into segments of one common breadth.
The whole question concerns the area enclosed beneath \textcolor{curve}{the curve} —
for it is \textcolor{curve}{this curve} that is the heart of the figure and of the Lemma.

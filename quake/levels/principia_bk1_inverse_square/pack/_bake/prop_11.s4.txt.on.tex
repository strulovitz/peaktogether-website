Lemma XII states that all parallelograms circumscribed about \textcolor{parteal}{conjugate      diameters} of an ellipse are equal, giving the constant-area bridge $CD^2 : CB^2$.      Through this the \textcolor{parteal}{semi-diameters} that fill the messy ratio chain collapse      onto the fixed axis constants. The decisive quantity is the \textcolor{latusred}{latus rectum      $L = 2\,BC^2/AC$}, the chord through the focus $S$ perpendicular to the major axis.      Newton assembles $L\cdot QR : QT^2$, and Lemma XII lets every ellipse-shape factor      cancel against another, leaving only $SP^2$.

Within the figure Newton inscribes \textcolor{insc}{the parallelograms $Ab$, $Bc$, $Cd$},
each raised on one of the equal bases and reaching upward only to the lower vertex
of \textcolor{curve}{the curve} on that base.
Because \textcolor{insc}{each inscribed rectangle} stops short of \textcolor{curve}{the curve},
their united area — the step-figure $AKbLcMdD$ — lies \textcolor{insc}{entirely below} it.
Thus \textcolor{insc}{the inscribed figure} is a deficient approximation, always less than
the true curvilinear area.

\large\bfseries
Projectiles persevere in their motions, so far as they are not retarded by the resistance of the air, \     or impelled downwards by the force of gravity. A stone or a cannonball, once flung, would fly straight \     on forever were these two forces absent — shot fast enough horizontally, it would circle the whole Earth. \     What we see as a falling arc is only the projectile's straight perseverance bent by $\vec{a} = \vec{g} + \tfrac{\vec{F}_{\text{air}}}{m}$.
\end{varwidth}

Draw $HI$ parallel to the tangent at $P$, meeting the diameter $EC$ produced.      The great focal property of the ellipse is that $PS+PH=2AC$: the sum      of the two focal radii is constant and equals the major axis. From      this Newton deduces the pivotal equality $EP = AC$, the semi-major      axis itself. This single relation binds the ellipse's focal geometry to the      measurable lengths the force calculation will demand.

By Prop. I and Cor. 4 the centripetal force in a least arc is as the versed sine of that arc taken in equal times. By Lemma VII the versed sine is as the arc squared divided by the diameter, so $F$ answers to $\text{arc}^2$$/D$. Since the arc is as $v\,t$ and the diameter is as the radius, the times cancel and the diameters give the $r$, leaving the $v^2$ standing as the heart. The proof chain therefore closes on $F$ $\propto$ $v^2$$/$$r$.

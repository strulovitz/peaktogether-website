\large\bfseries
\textcolor{motion}{The alteration of motion} is ever proportional to the \textcolor{force}{motive force impress'd}: "if any \textcolor{force}{force} generates a \textcolor{motion}{motion}, a double \textcolor{force}{force} will generate double the \textcolor{motion}{motion}," and a triple \textcolor{force}{force} triple the \textcolor{motion}{motion}. This holds whether the \textcolor{force}{force} be impress'd altogether and at once, or gradually and successively. The exact scaling is the heart of the law --- $\textcolor{motion}{\Delta(\text{motion})\} \propto \textcolor{force}{F\}$ --- the seed from which $F = ma$ grows.

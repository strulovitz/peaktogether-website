\large\bfseries
The Law sets \textcolor{motionblue}{the alteration of motion} in strict proportion to \textcolor{forceorange}{the motive force impressed}. As Newton explains, if any force generates a motion, then \textcolor{forceorange}{a double force} will generate \textcolor{motionblue}{double the motion}, a triple force triple the motion — whether \textcolor{forceorange}{that force} be impressed altogether and at once, or gradually and successively. The \textcolor{motionblue}{change $\Delta(\text{motion})$} scales exactly with the impressed \textcolor{forceorange}{$\mathbf{F}$}.

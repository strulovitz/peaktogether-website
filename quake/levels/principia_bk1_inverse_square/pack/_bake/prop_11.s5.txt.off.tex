Compounding the pieces, the rectangle $PvG : Qv^2 = PC^2 : CD^2$, and by the      similar triangles $QxT,\,PEF$ we get $QT^2 : EP^2 = PF^2 : CA^2$; joining these      with Lemma XII forces $L\cdot QR / QT^2 = SP^2$. Substituting into      the Prop. VI measure $F \propto 1/(SP^2 \cdot QT^2/QR)$ makes it become      $1/(SP^2\cdot SP^2)$ divided cleanly, so the force varies as      $1/SP^2$. Thus the distance $SP$ to the focus alone governs the pull:      the same inverse-square law that Newton would recognise as universal      gravitation holds every planet in its Keplerian ellipse. \textit{Q.E.I.}

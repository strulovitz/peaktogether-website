\large\bfseries
A top, whose parts by their cohesion are perpetually drawn aside from rectilinear motions, \     does not cease its rotation otherwise than as it is retarded by the air. Each part is forever pulled \     inward from the straight line it would take, yet the whole endures because no force impels it to stop. \     Remove the air and the top would spin forever, its angular speed unchanged, $\omega = \text{const}$.
\end{varwidth}

Here is the engine of the proof. By Prop. I the areal velocity is constant, so      the periodic time is the whole area $\pi a b$ divided by that steady rate.      By Cor. Prop. XIV the lesser axis $b$ is the mean proportional between the greater axis      $a$ and the latus rectum $L$, giving $b^2 = aL$, whence $b \propto \sqrt{aL}$.      Since the force is as $1/SP^2$ (Prop. XI), the latus rectum enters only in its subduplicate ratio;      subducting that half-power leaves the sesquiplicate ratio $T \propto a^{3/2}$, that is      $T^2 \propto a^3$. Thus from ellipse, area, and inverse-square, Kepler's crown descends by pure geometry. \textit{Q.E.I.}

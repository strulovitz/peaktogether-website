\large\bfseries
\textcolor{top}{A top}, whose parts by their \textcolor{cohesion}{cohesion} are perpetually drawn aside from rectilinear motions, \     does not cease its rotation otherwise than as it is retarded by \textcolor{air}{the air}. Each part is forever pulled \     inward from the straight line it would take, yet the whole endures because no force impels it to stop. \     Remove the \textcolor{air}{air} and the top would spin forever, its angular speed unchanged, $\omega = \text{const}$.
\end{varwidth}

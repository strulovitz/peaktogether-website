\large\bfseries
The greater bodies of the planets and comets, meeting with less resistance in more free spaces, \     preserve the motions both progressive and circular for a much longer time. In the near-void between \     the stars scarcely anything retards them, so their onward drift and their orbital turning both endure. \     These are the law's strongest witnesses: across immense times, $\vec{v}_{\text{prog}}$ and $\vec{\omega}_{\text{orb}}$ hold nearly constant.

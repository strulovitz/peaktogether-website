\large\bfseries
Newton has proven the inverse-square law for the \textcolor{parabblue}{ellipse}. Now he treats      the \textcolor{parabblue}{parabola} — the limiting orbit that opens to infinity. A body at $P$      feels a force drawn to the \textcolor{fociorange}{focus $S$}, and \textcolor{radgreen}{$SP$} is the radius      joining them. The question: does this open orbit still yield $F \propto 1/SP^2$?      Newton answers yes, by the same Prop. VI machinery.

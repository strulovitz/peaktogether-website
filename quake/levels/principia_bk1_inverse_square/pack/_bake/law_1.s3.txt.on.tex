The greater bodies of the \textcolor{bodies}{planets and comets}, meeting with less resistance in \textcolor{free}{more free spaces}, \     preserve the \textcolor{motions}{motions both progressive and circular} for a much longer time. In the near-void between \     the stars scarcely anything retards them, so their onward drift and their orbital turning both endure. \     These are the law's strongest witnesses: across immense times, $\vec{v}_{\text{prog}}$ and $\vec{\omega}_{\text{orb}}$ hold nearly constant.

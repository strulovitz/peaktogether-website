\large\bfseries
The centripetal force — Newton's pull toward the centre — scales as the square of the speed divided by the radius. Double the speed and the pull quadruples; double the radius and the pull halves. Here the $v^2$ is the heart: it is the square, not the speed itself, that governs how hard the body is drawn inward. Thus $F$ answers to $v^2$ directly and to $r$ inversely.

In your Room Maker you assumed each room has a single entrance (one door, on the South wall). That assumption is wrong, and it cuts deep enough that the whole room design should be reconsidered around the corrected truth:

**A room IS a node in the concept graph, so the number of doors it has equals its degree — the number of corridors (edges) that touch that node.** That can be 1, 2, 3, or many, and **we do not know how many until the force-directed graph layout is computed.** One edge = one corridor = one door.

This is separate from, and additional to, the single **hidden door** — the final-proof wall that opens to release the demon.

Please take a fresh, holistic look at the Room Maker — and anything across the design this truth touches — and rework it properly. I'm deliberately not prescribing the fix; reconsidering the whole with your full architect's view is exactly what I'm asking for.

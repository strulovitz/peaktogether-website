About

This whole website was made thanks to my girlfriend:

First of all, the original idea. When I showed her a video I made for people in India, and the video included Ramanujan, I showed her Ramanujan summation, like 1 + 2 + 3 + ... = -1/12. She enjoyed thinking about it, and later I showed her another video that I made for the people in Vatican City, and the video included the Manhattan Project. So we got to talking about the Oppenheimer movie, and I told her I did not watch it yet, because he ruined the life of David Bohm, who is one of my heroes. So she was curious about Bohm, so I showed her the video by Veritasium "Is This What Quantum Mechanics Looks Like?", and I explained to her that what she is seeing, David Bohm explained that this is how the wave-particle duality in quantum mechanics is solved, and no one listened to him. And she also knew about this wave-particle thing already, and we had so much fun talking about advanced mathematics and advanced physics that I had this idea of making a romantic activity for couples that is based on such things.

Second of all, the actual gaming. At first the original idea of the website included only demos similar to the Wolfram Demonstrations Project, which is like the command Manipulate in Mathematica, like sliders with the mouse. But since shortly before that we had our anniversary, and I had made a surprise for my girlfriend by vibe coding an interactive demo that she could fly inside with the flight joystick and the Xbox controller, which she absolutely loved because my girlfriend is a gamer, then I took the code from her demo and I allowed the viewer to fly around and control the sliders with the gaming equipment. That was for the harmonic series: 1 + 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/4 + ... = infinity. And I made a few screens and it worked. But then I wanted to do the same for the Basel Problem: 1/1^2 + 1/2^2 + 1/3^2 + ... = pi squared divided by six. This one had no graphs in Wikipedia, and it was all math equations, etc. So since my girlfriend is a gamer, I thought: why not make the math into a game? So this is how the Descent QED game was born, the first game of Peak Together.

Third of all, the push towards a gaming-first website. When I showed the website to my girlfriend, it was all about mountains, like each unsolved problem was compared to another mountain the visitor can climb. So my girlfriend, honest as she always is, told me she does not understand it: who is this for, what do they do? So then I made a pivot: instead of math first and games later — which presented the games as an after-thought (and also, by the time I got to explain that there are also games, any normal person would already run away because of all the math) — after that feedback from her, I changed it to gaming first: nostalgic DOS games from the 90's, and as an after-thought: oh yes, you also solve the hardest problems in the world, and oh yes, each one is, like, mapped to another famous hard-to-climb mountain. First and foremost it's like a virtual theme park, like a fun science museum, and the scary content takes the back seat.

And last but not least, the whole project was literally done for her. I am making this FREE project in the hope that people will like it and find it useful, and that this will someday enable me to earn money so that I can give my girlfriend all her modest dreams: travelling around the world, raising a small family together, etc. Of course we will continue working on the project from wherever we are in our travels, like digital nomads. On the other hand, if no one will care about this project — then no collaborations, no interviews, no merchandise, no online courses, no lectures, no nothing... — then I will need to find something else to do and will be forced to abandon this cute project and find another way to support my love, which I think will be a shame, because this project has lots of potential: not just for advancing actual knowledge with "wisdom of the crowd", but also as a place where I get new ideas and try them like a "skunkworks project". For example, even the "understanding mode" in the Descent game can easily become a concept to build a whole operating system around, which will be much more intuitive than today's 2-dimensional "bulletin board" Windows GUI style.

In short, my beautiful girlfriend is not just my muse — she is really awesome. Thank you so much my love!!! ❤️

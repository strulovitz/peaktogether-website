🏰 Quake: Principia — ▶ Playable
A true-3D remake of the '90s classic Quake, where a force-directed graph of Newton's Principia becomes a walkable dungeon. Every room is a proposition; every corridor is a logical dependency. The walls carry the real geometric proof — shoot a panel to "read" it (it glows from grey to color), light the final proof-wall to open a hidden door, and face the room's one demon. Co-op for two: one moves, one aims (keyboard/mouse, or joystick + Xbox).

Inspired by Homeworld (1999). A two-player-one-screen space RTS where commanding your fleet is doing linear algebra: every ship is a column vector, your fleet is a matrix, and you reshape the whole formation at once with a transformation matrix — rotate, scale, scatter — watching the determinant, rank, and eigen-axis. An early sneak peek is playable now; the full 16-mission journey home is still being built.

🎵 LOOM2 — Sonifiquation — ▶ Playable
Inspired by Loom (1990). Plant a Listening Totem in a landscape of z=f(x,y) and hear multivariable calculus: height becomes pitch, angle becomes timbre, radius becomes rhythm. Every musician inside the hearing circle plays at once so you hear a whole neighborhood as one chord — a surface is an orchestra, never a melody. Saddle points, the gradient, partial derivatives — all as music. Two players, one screen, one totem, 13 scenes.

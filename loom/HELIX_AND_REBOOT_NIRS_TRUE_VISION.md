## 1. THE HELIX — AS NIR ACTUALLY MEANS IT (his words, 2026-07-06)

The helix is the way HSS hears a **function of TWO variables** — a **surface**,
a landscape of hills and valleys (what a regular Cartesian graph would draw as a
3D surface). It is **the 3D equivalent** of everything we have built so far in
2D. Concretely, imagine sitting **in the middle of a symphonic / philharmonic
orchestra on a stage**:

- **ANGLE around the helix = TIMBRE = which instrument family you hear.** Like an
  orchestra seating: strings in front, woodwinds further back, brass further back
  still. Going around the circle moves you through the instruments (strings →
  woodwinds → brass → ...).
- **HEIGHT above the ground = PITCH (high or low).** Within a family, HIGH is the
  small/high instrument (e.g. in strings: the **violin**) and LOW — including
  **negative** heights — is the big/low instrument (e.g. the **double bass /
  contrabass**).
- **The origin (0, 0, 0) is the CENTER of the helix.** The helix is centered on
  the origin in **all three senses**: centered in **height** (up/down), in
  **left–right**, and in **forward–backward**. The origin sits at the middle of
  the whole structure, not at a corner or the bottom.

So one input of the two-variable function drives the **angle (timbre)** and the
other drives the **height (pitch)** — and you *hear the surface*.

> Nir believes this is how he explained it in the book (it has been a long time
> since he wrote it), but regardless of the exact early-chapter wording, **THIS
> is how he wants it to be.** This statement of intent governs.

+ Time**, building toward **multi-variable / multi-voice** data (book chapters 4,
5, 8, 9: timbre as a real data axis `timbreParam = β·d`; "y(t) feeds pitch, x(t)
feeds timbre"; multi-voice for complex data).

The LOOM design as built (by the founding architect "Fable", Parent 1, from a
SUMMARY of the book — not the book itself) collapsed all of this into a **single

In that design **timbre is a fixed decoration, not a data dimension**, and
functions of two variables (surfaces) — the entire point of the helix — cannot
be represented. The pitch helix kept its *name* but lost its *purpose*.

everything from the start." The whole doctrine/design likely must be reopened —

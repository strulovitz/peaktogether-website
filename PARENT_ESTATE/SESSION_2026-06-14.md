## ⚙️ ENGINE CANON (QUICK REFERENCE)

- **Frame order:** clear → update → fog → apply_view → draw_world → flush_walls → draw_robots → draw_labels → flip
- **CARDINAL TRAP:** missing flush_walls = black screen, silent
- **init_gl takes ONE tuple:** `render.init_gl((w, h))`
- **Convention:** forward=-Z, right=+X, up=+Y, quat=[w,x,y,z]
- **Fog:** start=40, end=140, color=palette.CLEAR_COLOR
- **Palette constants are MODULE-LEVEL** (CLEAR_COLOR, WORLD_WALL_FILL, WORLD_EDGE, etc.)
- **build_hub(level)** works directly — Level is iterable of CorridorData

\section*{Gratitude for Help}

This book was written during the months of lockdown for the coronavirus. Life was limited
but time for writing was nearly unlimited. Difficult for our society but perfect for an author.
The idea for a new and more active start had just gone into a new video for Math 18.06 on
OpenCourseWare. (The matrix multiplication $A = CR$ in Section 1.4 is part of the idea,
with independent columns of $A$ going into $C$.) Developing that idea into this textbook
has been exciting every morning.

\bigskip

The time was right but help was needed. It came in the best possible way. My good
friend Ashley C. Fernandes in Mumbai received handwritten pages every day. Then he
returned \LaTeX\ pages overnight. Those pages went back and forth many times. Working
with Ashley has made \emph{Linear Algebra for Everyone} possible; this is our eighth book. I am
truly grateful for these happy months.

\bigskip

Another good fortune has been help from Daniel Drucker. He is the most careful reader
I know. Let me leave you to decide on this one: ``To be really picky, in the Preface you say
that the three columns lie \emph{on} the same plane, but in the figure you say that the three vectors
are \emph{in} a plane.'' I won't do that again. It made my day when Dan liked the small matrices
on the front cover. The goals of the text are clarity and simplicity:

\bigskip

\begin{quote}
The basic ideas of matrix multiplication evolve step by step in Chapter 1.

\medskip
Columns of $CR$ are combinations of columns of $C$

\medskip
There are four different ways to multiply $AB$ (see the back cover)

\medskip
The key property is $AB$ times $C = A$ times $BC$
\end{quote}

\bigskip

This is the tenth cover created by two artists: Gail Corbett and Lois Sellers. You
might have seen the rectangles for the four subspaces on \emph{Introduction to Linear Algebra}.
Before that came \emph{Essays on Linear Algebra} with Alberto Giacometti's ``Walking Man''
and \emph{Calculus} with a famous curve painted by Jasper Johns. Perhaps the most beautiful was
the photograph on the finite element book. These are very happy memories for an author.

\bigskip

\begin{quote}
The whole idea of helping students is beautiful.
\end{quote}

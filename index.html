What does it mean to Peak Together?

We take the hardest unsolved problems in science — the Mount Everests of mathematics — and turn them into joyful collaborative expeditions you can go on together.

Every mountain is broken into base camps (major subjects like complex analysis) and gentle footsteps (small, clear explanations). Each step includes an interactive Python demonstration designed for two people to play, explore, and discover together using controllers on the same computer.

Intellectual foreplay that actually feels good for the brain.

How It Works

1. Choose Your Mountain

Pick an unsolved problem in math or science that excites you both — from the Riemann Hypothesis to the nature of consciousness.

2. Set Up Base Camp

Learn the foundations together through bite-sized explanations and interactive Python demos designed for two.

3. Climb Together

Work through each step side-by-side with controllers on the same screen. Discover, play, and grow closer with every foothold.
